# 🌦️ The Weather-App

**Your weather, your locations, your way — with AI.**

Stay up to date with the weather wherever you are! This app lets you check the weather for multiple locations, switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit, and choose between English and German. No complicated setup, no hassle — just the weather!

## ✨ Features

- 🌤️ **Live weather information** — Check the current weather and stay up to date with the latest conditions.
- 📍 **Multiple locations** — Add and manage multiple locations to keep an eye on the weather in different places.
- 🌡️ **Celsius & Fahrenheit** — Switch between °C and °F whenever you want.
- 🌍 **English & German** — Use the app in English or German.
- 💻 **Windows/PC support** — Run the app directly on your PC.
- 🤖 **Android support** — Download the APK and take your weather app on the go.
- 🏠 **Self-hosting** — Host the app on your own PC and access it on your local network.
- 🌐 **Web version** — Access the app through your browser without needing a separate desktop installation.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Choose whichever option works best for you:

- **🌐 Web:** [Open the Weather-App](https://totti-3.github.io/Weather-App) *(https://totti-3.github.io/Weather-App/)*

- **🤖 Android:** Download the latest Android APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/totti-3/Weather-App/releases/tag/Stable-Release) page.
- **🛠️ Self-hosting:** Download the latest PC version, run it on your computer, and access the app through your browser. Keep your PC running while you want to access your self-hosted instance.

## 🏠 Self-Hosting

Want to run your own weather app? No problem!

1. Download the latest version from the [Releases](https://github.com/totti-3/Weather-App/releases/tag/Stable-Release) page.
2. Run the application on your PC.
3. Open the provided local URL in your browser.
4. To access the app from your Android device, connect it to the same Wi-Fi network and open the PC's local network address.

**Note:** Remote access from outside your home network may require additional network configuration. Avoid exposing the app directly to the internet without appropriate security measures.
